# v0.7.0

Survival switch, poisonous fog, scuba gear, advanced binoculars, bounded
fleeing and flyer spawns, and the DHC OS field manual, island rules and CRT
pass. The full list is in [CHANGELOG.md](../../CHANGELOG.md).

## Highlights

- **Survival switch.** Survival is offered only when the game asks for it (a
  `survival` rules line, `c2-pc play --offer-survival`, or an APK-built game,
  where it is always on). Launcher: Survival mode on the instance editor's
  Base game page, off by default. Standalone games start survival on
  Mount Ravan when the stock animals are present, else on the last listed
  area. Survival waves now come in Genesis Redux, Fallen Kings and JPHL, and
  route round objects.
- **Poisonous fog.** AREA5's volcanic gas (mortal fog zones) drains health and
  kills the hunter while the eye is under the zone's top and above water.
- **Advanced binoculars (PC).** New gear. Animals are outlined through the
  binoculars, red while they are after the hunter.
- **Scuba gear.** New gear, offered per instance (`scuba_gear`). Faster diving,
  a longer underwater view and an oxygen tank that halves the drowning rate.
- **Bounded fleeing.** A scared animal waits out a lockout before it flees
  again when it still sees or smells the hunter. The lockout follows the
  hunter's movement on PC, Wii U and VR, and is fixed at 15 s on 3DS.
- **Flyers.** Pterosaurs and other flyers spawn inside their altitude band and
  never on the ground.
- **DHC OS field manual.** Lists every animal of the hunt setup with its file;
  areas and weapons have files too, with terrain and weapon models. The
  "DHC OS 3D specimens" option (off by default) turns the models. Records
  keep each kill's size.
- **DHC OS island rules and polish.** `_DHC.TXT` island rules apply in the
  original menus (PC, Android, 3DS hexagon menu, Wii U). The PC launcher
  recommends DHC OS once per instance with advanced settings. UI beeps and a
  CRT pass are added (`DHC OS sounds`, `dhc_crt_layers`).
- **Advanced DHC OS UI (PC, experimental, off by default).** A launcher switch,
  "Advanced DHC OS UI: Always On". HUNT SETUP then opens a map carousel with
  each map's relief, a survey panel, the hunter's record and the points.
- **Weapon hands.** The first-person weapon models in the armoury lose their
  arms, and `HUNTDAT/_HANDS.TXT` lets a mod keep or drop parts of a model.
  Hunts draw weapons as before.

## Compatibility

- New recipe keys and rules lines are optional. Recipes without them behave as
  before.
- 3DS and Wii U packs offer Survival only when cooked with `--offer-survival`.
  Recook a pack to get the Survival entry.
- `HUNTDAT/_HANDS.TXT` is optional. Models without one keep their current
  look apart from the armoury file.
