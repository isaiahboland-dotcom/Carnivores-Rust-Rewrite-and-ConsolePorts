# v0.6.0

Spawn ecosystem and awareness rules, bounded fleeing, the DHC OS terminal and
launcher look, modern graphics options, memory-planned 3DS hunts and Play
Vanilla. The full list is in [CHANGELOG.md](../../CHANGELOG.md).

## Highlights

- **Spawn ecosystem (experimental, PC and Android).** Recipes can carry
  `[settings.world]` zones, starts and extraction points, drawn in the
  launcher's new map editor, and `[settings.world.ecosystem]` rules for what
  appears where, by time of day, with spawn-safety distances and per-species
  and per-map caps. The rules steer the game's own placement and never remove
  an animal. Off by default.
- **Awareness (experimental, PC and Android).** `[settings.behavior.awareness]`
  adds suspicion and searching between calm and alarmed. Off by default.
- **Bounded fleeing (experimental).** Scared animals keep to the map and to the
  ground or water they live in; covers stock carnivores and Modder's Edition
  fish, flyers, brachiosaurs and ambient animals. Available on PC, 3DS, Wii U
  and VR; the regression runner can judge a flight.
- **Modern graphics (PC, all off by default).** Waves, depth-tinted water,
  sky glow, water reflections, sun through fog, underwater effects and
  desaturated night, plus fog zones that follow the map's own fog list and a
  "Pitch black night" option.
- **DHC OS.** The PC terminal gains pad navigation and a records page; the
  launcher follows the terminal look by default (Legacy remains); the 3DS
  opens the terminal by default. Missions, licences and hardcore hunt rules
  are recipe-editable; no game plays missions yet.
- **3DS.** A hunt's memory is planned: picked animals first, then secret,
  ambient and aquatic animals as far as memory allows. Packs must be cooked
  again (pack 5); older packs load as before.
- **Play Vanilla** from the launcher and `c2-pc play --recipe R --classic`,
  with recipe validation warnings.
- **Trophy room.** A saved layout is used only on the map it was made for.

## Compatibility

- 3DS packs cooked before this release still load; recompile them to use the
  memory plan.
- New recipe keys are optional. Recipes without them behave as before.
