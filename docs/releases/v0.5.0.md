# v0.5.0

Regression scenarios, instance rules and the first Android build

## Highlights

- **Gameplay regression scenarios** (`c2-regress`, developer tool): a scripted,
  deterministic hunt that loads an area, places four animals, fires every weapon once
  and opens the binoculars, map and pickers. Runs record functional results,
  per-checkpoint simulation hashes, screenshots and timings, and compare against a
  baseline. Published vanilla baselines for Carnivores 2, Ice Age and both APK-built
  bases are in the repository; the same script also runs in the original engines under
  Wine. [docs/REGRESSION.md](../REGRESSION.md) explains how to use it to validate fixes
  and optimizations, and what a passing run does not prove. Pull requests now ask for
  the results.
- **Instance rules** (launcher, Advanced tab; off by default): which base-game areas,
  animals and weapons are kept, which times of day are offered, settings locked to a
  value, and spawn rules (caps per area, secret animals). Ammo per weapon on the Balance
  tab. Recipes keep all of it.
- **Predation** (experimental, all platforms; off by default): carnivores hunt
  herbivores lower on the food chain, and prey runs from them.
- **Android** (first build): the PC game in an APK, for a game built from your own
  mobile APK plus add-ons, with the phone game's touch controls, controller support and
  a GPU performance pass. The launcher's **Build > Android** packages and installs it
  over adb.
- **PC video**: a foliage distance slider and an optional high-end view (render
  distance up to the whole map, coarser far ground, model-to-sprite fading).
- **Hardcore survival** (PC; off by default): endless night with flashlight, radar and
  shotgun.
- **Parity**: the original's wind indicator and compass on the hunt view, and its 16 px
  hunt-setup rows.

The full list is in the [changelog](../../CHANGELOG.md).

## Validation and limits

- Linux PC is the gameplay baseline. The regression scenario passes on Carnivores 2,
  Ice Age and both APK-built bases, headless and rendered, with identical repeats.
- The scenarios cover one area and four animals on PC only. Menus, the launcher, mod
  merging and the console builds are covered by unit tests and hand-played checks, not
  by the scenarios.
- Android has been run on one device; phones and pause/resume still need testing.
- Predation is least tested on 3DS, Wii U, Android and VR, and can get chaotic where
  many animals spawn close together.
- The 3DS and Wii U builds have not had fresh hardware runs for this release's
  features. Windows remains experimental.
