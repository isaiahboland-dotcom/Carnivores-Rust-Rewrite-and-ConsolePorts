# v0.5.2

Launch protection fails closed, predation fixes, kill-animation scenarios, first Old 3DS readings!

## Fixed

- **Launch protection.** A recipe whose base game hash list is missing, broken or empty
  no longer plays unchecked: the launcher blocks it with no override and the command line
  fails. A base that differs from the recipe stops `c2-pc play --recipe`, `recipe build`
  and `recipe verify` unless `--accept-base` is given; it never covers a broken list or a
  wrong APK base. The launcher remembers **Use this game anyway** per instance and game
  folder, so it isn't asked on every launch.
- **Predation** (experimental, off by default):
  - A carnivore that finished or was interrupted in a meal stood eating for the rest of
    the hunt. Every meal now ends on the species' walk clip and its AI takes over; the
    T-Rex, which eats with its sniff clip, is left to its own AI.
  - A carnivore shot dead next to its prey could still kill it, and a dead scavenger
    could still eat.
  - The population only went down. An animal killed by another lies 60 s for
    scavengers, then comes back as a new animal of its kind, out of the hunter's sight.
    The hunter's own kills don't come back, and a predator's kill still scores nothing.
- **Pick limit.** With `--max-picks` set (Android), a refused pick shows
  **Limit reached** instead of doing nothing.
- **Android packages.** The Android tab warns when two instances' phone names give the
  same package, and install refuses.
- **Recipe `skip`.** A `skip` of 0 is rejected when read from a file, as it already was
  on the command line.
- The Android manifest had stayed at 0.4.3; it now matches the workspace, and a test
  fails if they drift.

## Gameplay regression scenarios (developer tool)

- A comparison fails when the scenario file's text differs, and repeats that disagree
  fail the run. `publish` requires agreeing repeats and the committed scenario text, and
  won't overwrite a baseline without `--replace`. The four vanilla baselines were
  re-recorded (three repeats); their checkpoint state is unchanged.
- A visual check that compared no pictures no longer counts as a pass. `--same-state`
  fails a comparison on differing checkpoint state. The rendered pass's checkpoint hashes
  must match the headless run's.
- New `kill_animations` scenario: each animal is released toward the hunter and must
  kill it with the right death, a body clip from the hunter model, both models still
  animating, and the body staying with its killer. Covers Carnivores 2, Ice Age and three
  modded packs. It also runs against the original engines, where Carnivores 2 matches
  clip for clip; Ice Age's synchronised kill is not ported yet.

## Nintendo 3DS

First readings on an original 2DS and an Old 3DS XL for ten Old 3DS packs, in
[`3ds-ver/README.md`](../../3ds-ver/README.md#hardware-results-2026-11-08). Most packs
hold about 30 fps with animals in view; Ice Age APK reaches 45 fps and Anachronous 57 fps
with none(although Anachronous dips down towards mid-20's when dinosaurs are in view). 
Trees as sprites raised fps and lowered GPU command-buffer use in every
comparison. The optimisation plan that follows from these readings is in
[ROADMAP.md](../ROADMAP.md#3ds-performance-plan).

## Validation and limits

- PC workspace formatting, Clippy with warnings denied (default and all features) and
  locked tests pass, as do the cooker's tests.
- The predation respawn was checked with the 1800 s predation parity run: Area 1 kept
  25 of 25 animals (was 8) and Area 3 21 of 21 (was 12). Unit tests cover the meal
  hand-back for Velociraptor, Allosaurus and T-Rex.
- The 3DS readings are spot readings from the in-game overlay, not averages, and spots
  and animal counts differ between them. On the 3DS, JPHL's AREA4 and AREA5 don't start
  even with minimal picks, and Genesis Redux doesn't start a hunt; both are open.
- The predation and launch-protection changes have not been re-checked on Windows, the
  Steam Deck or Android hardware.
